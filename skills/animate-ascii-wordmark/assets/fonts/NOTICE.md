# Bundled fonts

Unmodified Geist and Geist Mono WOFF2 subsets, by The Geist Project Authors.
Source: https://github.com/vercel/geist-font (via @fontsource-variable/geist and @fontsource-variable/geist-mono).

Geist: Latin, Latin Extended and Vietnamese. Geist Mono: Latin (the rendered glyphs are ASCII).
Each family retains its own SIL Open Font License 1.1 in this directory.
